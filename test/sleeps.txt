# Every sleep in ear's tests and their support, and every Catch WARN or CHECK_NOFAIL, with its role. The sleep guard
# (test/sleep_guard.cmake, the CTest test ear_sleeps_are_listed) fails on one that is not listed here, on a listing
# that matches no line, and on a role a line may not have; docs/testing.md#the-sleep-guard is the rule.
#
# One line each: <role> | <path from the repository root> | <the line, trimmed, exactly as it stands> | <why>
# A line that occurs twice in a file is listed twice. Editing a listed line, even its comment, means editing its entry:
# that is the review the guard asks for.
#
# Roles of a sleep:
#   induce    the sleep is the event: a stall, a hold or a slow step the test makes on purpose.
#   negative  a bounded look for something that must not happen, taken after the event that would have caused it.
#   poll      a pause between looks at the event a test waits for, which a deadline only fails (the look decides).
#   pace      the clock of a stand-in for something that keeps real time: a sender, a heartbeat, a storm's rate.
#   luck      it stands in for an event, or the test passes only if the host is quick: owed to the audit
#             (docs/design/root-classes.md#53-the-audit), and names the step that removes it (R5b, R5c or R5d).
# Roles of a WARN or CHECK_NOFAIL, which tells and goes on where a test should fail naming its event:
#   luck      it goes on when the host did not do what the test needed (owed, naming its step).
#   owed      any other telling-and-going-on: a skip, or a report that belongs in INFO (owed, naming its step).
